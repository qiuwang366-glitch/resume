# Global Macro Outlook: Navigating Fed Policy in 2025

The global fixed income landscape continues to present both challenges and opportunities as we navigate through 2025. In this analysis, I explore key themes shaping portfolio positioning.

## Fed Policy Trajectory

The Federal Reserve's approach to rate normalization remains a critical driver for duration positioning. Key considerations include:

- **Terminal rate expectations**: Markets are pricing in 3-4 rate cuts by year-end
- **Balance sheet runoff**: QT continues at a measured pace, impacting Treasury supply dynamics
- **Labor market signals**: Wage growth moderation suggests reduced inflation pressure

### Duration Strategy Implications

Given the current environment, our base case favors:

1. Modest duration extension in high-quality sovereigns
2. Curve steepener positions ahead of easing cycle
3. Tactical allocation to TIPS as real rate compression accelerates

## Cross-Border Opportunities

## Risk Considerations

- Geopolitical tensions remain elevated
- Credit spreads near historical tights warrant caution
- Liquidity conditions may deteriorate during stress episodes

## Conclusion

Maintaining disciplined risk management while capturing asymmetric opportunities remains our core investment philosophy. The current environment rewards active management and cross-market expertise.
